Aclaración sobre el volumen de contenido:
Clarification on content volume:

El volumen de contenido indicado en cada paquete (posts, reels, stories, carruseles, etc.) representa el total de piezas producidas al mes. Estas piezas se adaptan al formato de cada red social incluida en el paquete y se distribuyen entre ellas — el volumen no se multiplica por cada red adicional, ni se divide en partes iguales entre ellas. El community management y los reportes de KPI sí se aplican de forma independiente por cada red social incluida.

The content volume indicated in each package (posts, reels, stories, carrousels, etc.) represents the total number of pieces produced monthly. These pieces are adapted to the format of each social network included in the package and distributed among them — the volume is not multiplied per additional network, nor is it divided equally among them. Community management and KPI reporting do apply independently for each included social network.
